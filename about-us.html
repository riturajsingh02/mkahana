The Makhanam Foundation

Built at the Source in Bihar to Solve
Global Sourcing Challenges.

Over 85% of the world’s Makhana originates in the tranquil, mineral-abundant wetlands of Mithila, Bihar. Yet for decades, international food manufacturers and domestic distributors struggled with fragmented brokers, erratic sizing, and volatile moisture levels.

Makhanam was founded to change that reality permanently. By combining generational harvesting traditions with modern industrial sorting, calibrated caliper systems, and hygienic post-harvest drying, we deliver raw foxnuts that meet the exact physical and microbiological specifications of the world's most demanding food companies.

Our Mission

To be the world’s most trusted single-origin processor of raw Makhana by championing farmgate transparency, rigorous grading, and dependable international logistics.

Our Vision

To elevate Bihar’s indigenous lotus crop onto global retail shelves, snacking brands, and nutraceutical formulations with uncompromised purity and ethical trade.

The Makhanam Advantage

Four Pillars of B2B Excellence

Why national snack brands, international importers, and wholesale distributors rely on our Bihar facility.

1. Direct Pondgate Origin

100% single-origin raw Foxnut harvested from Bihar's perennial wetlands, bypassing intermediary traders for maximum freshness and lot-by-lot traceability.

2. Millimeter Caliper Grading

Standardized via authentic Suta sizing (3 Suta to 7 Suta / 14mm to 22mm+), guaranteeing uniform batch diameters for consistent roasting and packaging.

3. Moisture Preservation

Strict low-moisture control (<8-10%) prevents sogginess, eliminates transit mold risk, and preserves signature crispness for 12+ months of export shelf life.

4. Container-Ready Freight

Flexible FOB/CIF contracts, food-grade poly-lined sacks (5kg, 10kg, 20kg), and phytosanitary certification for seamless customs clearance worldwide.
