THE ORIGIN

It begins in Mithila.

Makhana has been cultivated for generations across the calm freshwater ponds and natural wetlands of northern Bihar.

In Mithila, the harvest is rooted in deep generational knowledge—understanding the seasons, the water, and the precise moment seeds are ready. MAKHANAM was established directly in this landscape to respect and preserve this heritage while bringing dependable sourcing and consistent grading to businesses across India and global markets.

SEED TO SHIPMENT

The 4-Stage Quality Lifecycle

Visualizing how every single Foxnut is cultivated, harvested, expanded, and calibrated under strict standards.

Stage 01

Wetland Cultivation

Naturally grown in mineral-rich standing waters of northern Bihar without synthetic growth agents.

Stage 02

Underwater Harvest

Hand-gathered from muddy pond beds by skilled generational Mallah divers, then thoroughly water-cleaned.

Stage 03

Artisanal Popping

High-heat roasting and precise manual striking expand the dark shell into delicate, snow-white Makhana.

Stage 04

Caliper Grading

Multi-deck mechanical sieving categorizes lots from 3 Suta Pearl to 7 Suta Super Jumbo before sealed pack.
