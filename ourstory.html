THE ORIGIN

It begins in Mithila.

Makhana has been cultivated for generations across the calm freshwater ponds and natural wetlands of northern Bihar.

In Mithila, the harvest is rooted in deep generational knowledge—understanding the seasons, the water, and the precise moment seeds are ready. MAKHANAM was established directly in this landscape to respect and preserve this heritage while bringing dependable sourcing and consistent grading to businesses across India and global markets.
