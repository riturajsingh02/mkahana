Origin & Processing

From Pond to Precision Caliper

How Bihar's harvest becomes standardized export-grade Makhana through traditional roasting and modern optical machines.

Stage 1 • Roasting & Puffing

Wood-Fired Cast Iron Popping

Hard aquatic seeds are roasted at calibrated high heat and hand-popped on wooden anvils to expand into snow-white Phool Makhana.

Stage 2 • Optical Color Sorting

Automated Color Separation

High-speed cameras identify and discard discolored or bruised kernels to maintain uniform bright ivory color purity across every export batch.

Stage 3 • Caliper Suta Grading

Multi-Deck Mesh Rotary Sizing

Trommel sieves sort nuts cleanly into 7 Suta, 6+ Suta, 5 Suta, 4 Suta, and 3 Suta categories before final manual conveyor inspection.
